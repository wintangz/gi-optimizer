# Research: Master Elemental Resonance Toggle (`resonance-master-toggle`)

## Decision 1: Mechanism for Complete Elemental Resonance Suppression in GO Calc Graph

- **Decision**: When `resonanceEnabled === false`, `hydratePresetTeam` sets `team.conditional.resonance` to `{ __masterOff: 'off' }` (and includes `resonanceEnabled` in `presetTeamFingerprint`), and `getEffectiveResonanceData` in `packages/engine/src/teamData.ts` returns `{}` (`{} as Data`) whenever `resCond?.__masterOff === 'off' || resCond?.all === 'off'`.
- **Rationale**:
  1. In `@genshin-optimizer/gi/sheets` (`Resonance.tsx`), `resonanceData` contains all team-wide resonance buffs (`teamBuff.premod` for Pyro `atk_`, Hydro `hp_`, Anemo `staminaDec_`/`moveSPD_`/`cdRed_`, Geo `shield_`/`all_dmg_`/`geo_enemyRes_`, Dendro `eleMas`, Protective Canopy `*_res_`, and `teamBuff.total.critRate_` for Cryo `ShatteringIce`).
  2. Returning `{}` from `getEffectiveResonanceData` (which is wrapped in `inferInfoMut(..., 'Resonance')` in `getCharDataBundle`) removes every resonance node from the character's `Data[]` array, including the small-team (< 4 members) resonance synthesis path (`smallTeamEleCounts`).
  3. Because no `Data` node with `source: 'Resonance'` is added to the calc graph when `resonanceEnabled === false`, `extractDamageRowBreakdown` and `extractStatBreakdowns` in `packages/engine/src/breakdown.ts` naturally emit zero `category: 'resonance'` (`Elemental Resonance`) contributors without special-case post-filtering.
  4. When `resonanceEnabled` is `true` or `undefined` (default), the code path in `hydratePresetTeam` and `getEffectiveResonanceData` is unchanged bit-for-bit, guaranteeing zero golden movement.
- **Alternatives Considered**:
  - Deleting individual stat keys in `getEffectiveResonanceData`: More brittle and could miss Protective Canopy (`*_res_`) or leave empty `teamBuff` wrappers in the calc graph. Returning `{}` when master resonance is off is cleaner and complete.

## Decision 2: Per-Preset Persistence Model in `OptimizerSettings`

- **Decision**: Store per-preset master resonance overrides in `OptimizerSettings.resonanceEnabledByPreset?: Record<string, boolean>` (with `DEFAULT_OPTIMIZER_SETTINGS.resonanceEnabledByPreset = {}`), plus optional `resonanceEnabled?: boolean` on `OptimizerSettings` and `BuildSelection` for direct programmatic callers.
- **Rationale**: Matches the exact persistence pattern of `memberConditionalOverrides` and `memberBuildModes` in `packages/presets/src/settings.ts` and `useOptimizerSettings` in `apps/web/src/lib/storage/useGood.ts`. Sub-preset IDs generated by `buildMemberSubPreset` (`${preset.id}__member_${memberKey}`) fall back to the parent `preset.id` in `getPresetResonanceEnabled`.
- **Alternatives Considered**:
  - Storing inside `memberConditionalOverrides`: `memberConditionalOverrides` is keyed by `characterKey -> sheetKey -> condKey`, whereas Elemental Resonance is a team-level setting independent of any single character tab or per-member reset button.
